# Acknowledgments

The data comes from the Future Factories lab at the University of South Carolina:

R. Harik, F. El Kalach, J. Samaha, D. Clark, D. Sander, P. Samaha, L. Burns, I. Yousif, V. Gadow,
T. Tarekegne and N. Saha, "Analog and Multi-modal Manufacturing Datasets Acquired on the Future
Factories Platform", [arXiv:2401.15544](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15544) (2024). Data:
[Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ramyharik/ff-2023-12-12-analog-dataset), CC BY-SA 4.0.

The paper states that the work was funded in part by NSF Award 2119654, "RII Track 2 FEC: Enabling
Factory to Factory (F2F) Networking for Future Manufacturing", and by the South Carolina Research
Authority.

The pipeline uses NumPy, pandas, Apache Arrow, Apache Kafka (optional) with the confluent-kafka
client, and the Kaggle API client.
